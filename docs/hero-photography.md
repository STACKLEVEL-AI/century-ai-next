## Deployment generation brief

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original panoramic website hero photograph for CENTURY, an enterprise AI implementation company. Create a sophisticated editorial photograph, landscape 16:9, not a web mockup. Scene: a working session in a contemporary European engineering office, photographed from outside a glass meeting room across a long oak table. Three adult engineers in smart casual light shirts quietly review a laptop and discuss a simple process on a distant whiteboard, on the RIGHT third of the frame, with faces small and natural, not facing camera. A fourth colleague crosses the foreground at the far LEFT as an elegantly blurred silhouette. Medium-wide 35mm composition; believable work, candid expressions, imperfect lived-in details. The broad central 50 percent of the image is calm, soft-focus dark warm-grey glass reflections and architecture, deliberately clear of prominent faces, high-contrast edges, or legible screens so a large centered white two-line headline will read beautifully there. Camera slow shutter creates subtle horizontal motion blur in foreground only; main team on right is recognizable and reasonably sharp. Light: soft late-afternoon daylight from lateral windows, restrained highlights, natural photographic exposure, refined film grain, luminous rather than murky. Colors: warm grey, ivory, charcoal, light natural wood, one quiet blue notebook; no dominant green color cast. The top edge is calm and darker for a white navigation bar. This should look like thoughtful premium editorial art direction of real people working, not generic stock-photo posing or a glossy corporate render. Entirely original fictional adults, do not reproduce real people. No words, text, typography, logos, symbols, borders, watermark, fake software UI or graphic overlays. Produce only the photograph.

## Transformation generation brief

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: original landscape website hero photograph for CENTURY enterprise AI transformation. Create only a realistic, cinematic architectural editorial photograph in 16:9 landscape. A contemporary European company seen across the interior of a generous double-height office atrium: understated pale stone, warm grey concrete, slender black window frames, a long sculptural staircase toward the right, workspaces visible through glazing. Several adult colleagues walking between workspaces, small in the architecture, one naturally motion-blurred adult crossing the bottom left foreground and a pair discussing a folder at the far right. Human organization in motion; believable working day, no posing or direct camera gaze. Wide horizontal camera composition from a slightly elevated landing, thoughtful architectural perspective, restrained 35mm film quality, softly diffused afternoon daylight, warm silver-grey and ivory tones, a little muted amber wood, subtle blue clothing accent. Intentionally leave the broad CENTER UPPER-MIDDLE area as quiet, mid-dark softly reflected glass and charcoal wall planes with no prominent face, complex lines, or bright light, so a large centered white two-line website headline remains highly legible without an oppressive dark overlay. The bottom third and outer edges can contain architectural detail, reflections, and life. A calm darker top band supports a white navigation bar. Avoid dominant green, black empty void, futuristic science-fiction, neon, robots, cliché holograms, glowing neural networks, commercial stock-photography smiles, corporate team pose, full-screen close-up people. No text, signage, lettering, brand logos, watermark, graphic overlays or border. Entirely original fictional people and building.

## Deployment mobile generation brief

The generated Deployment desktop PNG was supplied as the only reference image.

Use case: photorealistic-natural, responsive art direction for an enterprise website. Reference image 1 is the original desktop hero photograph, used as a visual style and scene reference. Create a matching ORIGINAL PORTRAIT composition, 3:4 aspect ratio, for the mobile hero. Preserve the quiet European engineering office, oak table, glass reflections, warm charcoal and ivory photographic treatment, and the same three fictional colleagues reviewing a laptop. Recompose completely for portrait: the top 65 percent of the frame is calm, mid-dark glass and softly reflected office architecture, free of prominent people, faces, bright light fixtures, visible signage or complex high-contrast edges. In the LOWEST third of the frame place the three engineers at the table, medium wide, faces and gestures entirely below 66 percent of image height. A subtle blurred passerby at the extreme lower left adds documentary life. White website headline and paragraph will be added separately in the upper middle, so make that area visually quiet, dark but naturally photographic, not an artificial plain blank block. Soft side daylight illuminates the small team below, natural candid work and realistic hands. No text, logos, watermark, border or graphic overlays. Keep the original subdued editorial realism and avoid a dominant green cast. Output only the full-bleed portrait photograph.
